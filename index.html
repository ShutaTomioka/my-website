About Me

Originally from Kansai, now based in Sumida City, Tokyo. I hold a degree in Data Science from Kobe University (2025), where I specialized in aircraft fuel consumption optimization. Currently, I work as a Data Scientist at Kao Corporation, leveraging machine learning and advanced analytics to solve real-world problems.

Research & Projects

Academic research and professional applications of data science.

Aircraft Fuel Optimization (Thesis)

Topic: Reduction of fuel consumption through climb optimization using operational flight data.

Analyzed four months of flight data from Lufthansa Cargo's Boeing 777F to verify the effectiveness of switching from VNAV SPD to V/S mode during climb.

Result: Demonstrated a potential saving of 35-40 lbs per flight.

Last-Mile Logistics Optimization

Project: AEON NEXT "Senko Hanabi Coupon" Project.

Contributed to the source code development for optimizing delivery routes and coupon distribution for AEON's online supermarket, "Green Beans".
